# Code of conduct

Forge UI is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of working. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome
and able to do their best work. This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback, and take it with an open mind.
- Respect people's time. Search for existing issues before opening one, and keep discussions on topic.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new once.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language about a person's identity.
- Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code applies in the repository, in issues, pull requests, and discussions, and in any space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
reporting: open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately and
fairly, and retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.
